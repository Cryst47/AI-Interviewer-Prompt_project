FOUNDATION & MOTIVATION

Problem Statement & Industry Motivation

The Challenge with Traditional Prep

  • Static Question Banks: Traditional tools use static question lists that fail to adapt dynamically to candidate answers.
  • Absence of Actionable Feedback: Candidates receive binary pass/fail without deep rubric diagnostics on communication or STAR structuring.
  • High Human Coaching Costs: Professional human mock interviewers cost $150–$300/hour, creating steep accessibility barriers.
  • Spoken Anxiety Gap: Text-only drills leave candidates unprepared for real-world verbal pressure and pacing.

Our Prompt-Engineered Solution

  • Adaptive Multi-Turn Simulation: Generates escalating rigor questions tailored to role, seniority, and prior answers.
  • Multi-Persona Interviewers: Select from Supportive Mentor, Rigorous Tech Lead, and Demanding Executive personas.
  • Objective STAR Rubric Scoring: Measures Technical Depth, STAR Method, Communication, and Confidence (0-100).
  • Zero-Friction Multimodal Voice: Real-time browser Web Speech API transcription and natural voice synthesis.

PROMPT METHODOLOGY

Key Prompt Engineering Concepts Applied

1. Role Prompting

Explicit persona system directives enforce realistic behavioral quirks, domain vocabularies, and questioning philosophies without generic assistant drift.

2. Structured JSON Schemas

Forces the LLM to output valid JSON conforming strictly to predefined schemas, enabling deterministic parsing for charts and live scorecards.

3. Evaluation Prompts & Rubrics

Grounds evaluation in 4 orthogonal criteria (Technical, STAR, Clarity, Confidence) with mandatory quotes extracted from candidate answers.

4. Context Engineering

Feeds previous turn responses back into the prompt buffer to dynamically challenge candidate edge-case omissions in subsequent questions.

BENCHMARKS & RUBRICS

Evaluation Rubric & Optimization Results

Rubric Dimensions (0-100 Scale)

  • Technical Depth (35%): Accuracy, algorithmic complexity, architectural viability, trade-offs.
  • STAR Method (25%): Clear Situation, Task, Action, and Quantified Result narrative structure.
  • Communication & Clarity (20%): Concise delivery, professional terminology, zero filler.
  • Confidence & Alignment (20%): Executive presence, conviction, role-appropriate tone.

Prompt Optimization Comparison

  • Standard Zero-Shot: Generic compliments ("Good job!"), erratic scoring, frequent hallucinated claims.
  • Structured Rubric + Persona: 100% deterministic JSON, grounded evidence citations, 3.8x higher actionable learning utility.
